    ROBUST

    An AI-powered solution that automatically categorizes Health & Safety incident reports, saving analysts time and enabling data-driven decision making for safer workplaces.

    The Innovation

    Our Machine Learning powered tool automatically suggests Safety Area Categories in Incident and Accident reporting

    The Context

    Why We Built This

    The Problem

    Safety analysts currently spend significant time manually reviewing and correcting miscategorized incident reports. This repetitive work prevents them from focusing on meaningful analysis that could identify patterns, prevent future incidents, and improve workplace safety.

    Our Solution

    By leveraging Machine Learning technology, our tool automatically suggests Safety Areas in IKEAs Incident and Accidents reporting systems. This allows analysts to immediately begin their analysis with clean, accurate data, accelerating decision-making and improving overall safety outcomes.

    The Workflow

    From Report to Prediction

    Here is how the system processes data in real-time to assist safety analysts.

    How It Works

    1. 1 Coworker submits an Initial incident report
    2. 2 Incident description is sent to the ROBUST system
    3. 3 ROBUST produces a Safety Area prediction, along with a confidence score
    4. 4 The Safety Area prediction is displayed as the default option in the incident reporting system.
    5. 5 It is always possible for a coworker to change the Safety Area category, enabling human oversight.
