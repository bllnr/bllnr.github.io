    The Problem

    Safety analysts currently spend significant time manually reviewing and correcting miscategorized incident reports. This repetitive work prevents them from focusing on meaningful analysis that could identify patterns, prevent future incidents, and improve workplace safety.

    Our Solution

    By leveraging Machine Learning technology, our tool automatically suggests Safety Areas in IKEAs Incident and Accidents reporting systems. This allows analysts to immediately begin their analysis with clean, accurate data, accelerating decision-making and improving overall safety outcomes.
