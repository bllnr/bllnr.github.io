Workmate

A job search tool that does the legwork

Personal project
Full-stack web app | Year: 26

Project Idea

Much of a job search is busywork: checking various careers pages and job boards for new postings, keeping track of where you have applied, and reading lots of lengthy descriptions to find the few jobs that fit. Workmate takes care of that work, so the job seeker can spend their time on what actually moves them forward.

What would the job search look like with a tool that puts the job seeker's needs at the centre?

What It Does

  • Scans the careers pages of companies you are interested in for new postings
  • Tracks your ongoing applications
  • Reads experience levels, location and required skills from job posts
  • Scores how well a job matches your profile and helps prioritize
  • Includes a document editor for CVs and letters with tools to help you tailor applications for each role
  • Shows statistics, such as success rate and areas to improve

Design Principles

Privacy, empowerment and creativity

Workmate differs from other services for job seekers because the user controls their own data, have full insight into which jobs they see and can see how match scores are computed.


Workmate uses language models only where little creativity is needed and a lot of time is saved, such as reading a job posting and extracting its details. It never writes letters or CVs. That work is left to the user, whose own insight is what makes an application stand out.

Built with

Next.js, React and TypeScript, with a PostgreSQL database through Prisma. Careers pages are read with Playwright, and the app is tested with Vitest.
